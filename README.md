## What it does

`main.ts` writes a single original line — an alien tourist's review of
planet Earth, at most 200 characters — to `/output/entry.txt`. A judge
model then ranks the lines pairwise on funniness and originality.

The entry line (175 characters):

> Earth review: the dominant species obeys tiny furry dictators who shed everywhere, knock things off tables, and accept payment in treats. 4/5 stars — would be adopted again.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
